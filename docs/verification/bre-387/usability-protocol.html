Invitation draft — not sent

We are studying how people organize email in Orca. Would you be willing to try several tasks using a synthetic mailbox? We are evaluating the product, not you. Participation is optional; you can stop at any time. Before confirming, we will explain the session length, any recording, who can access observations and when they will be deleted.

Facilitator checklist

  1. Obtain owner approval and participant consent; use anonymous participant codes.
  2. Launch a fresh scratch mailbox per participant. Verify no real provider account or token is connected. Record code SHA, fixture, device, theme, browser version, and viewport.
  3. Read the task verbatim. Do not point at controls or name the implementation route. Start and stop timing only at approved boundaries.
  4. Record assistance, confusion, errors, and exact participant actions. Do not reinterpret assisted completion as independent success.
  5. Record preview latency separately from task completion time. Preserve failures and aborted sessions.
  6. Confirm removal left mail intact. Stop recording and follow approved retention rules.

Neutral tasks

  1. Keep project-related mail together so you can find it again and see future matching messages.
  2. Keep mail from the people who wrote these selected conversations together.
  3. This result includes a sender you did not intend. Adjust the result, then restore the prior result.
  4. Give the saved group a name you recognize, leave it, reload, and find it again.
  5. Remove the saved group while retaining its messages.

Do not say “Search → Save as View,” “Tune,” or “Undo” in uncoached task prompts. If asked for help, follow the approved coaching rule and record the request.
