Navigation

Inbox, Drafts and Settings are stable anchors. Compose is a persistent action, not an empty tab. Account selection changes all list and write contexts together. Back from a conversation restores the list; dismissing a composer preserves the draft unless the user explicitly discards it.

Completeness and exclusions

The inventory covers authentication, onboarding, lists, details, search, filtering, forms, account pickers, settings, feedback and overlays. Launch/loading is a state, not a destination. About/privacy information is reachable through Settings documentation. Payments, social profiles, discovery, desktop workspace/panels/tray and rule authoring do not apply to the first iPhone release. No separate in-app notification inbox: a notification opens its conversation.
