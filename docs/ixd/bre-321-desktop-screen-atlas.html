P00

Design contract

The system starts with what remains stable. Evidence explains placement; it does not become the primary navigation model.

Tidal control room

Calm surface.
Legible machinery.

Orca’s desktop is a programmable attention workspace. Stable destinations orient the person. Workflow spaces adapt around the work. Every automated placement can answer “why here?” without forcing evidence into the foreground.

Information hierarchy
1 · Current destination2 · Message or task3 · Contextual action4 · Evidence on request5 · System trace

Human Signal appears inside evidence and explanation—not as a permanent Human vs. Automated fork.

01Stable anchors

Inbox, Drafts, Organization, and Settings never move.

02User-shaped spaces

Focus, Signals, Quiet, Later, and custom spaces can be reordered, renamed, hidden, restored, or created.

03Evidence by context

“Why here?” opens beside the message. Trace and Audit go one level deeper.

04Recovery before blame

Drafts persist. Offline work is named. Every destructive action says what will happen next.
