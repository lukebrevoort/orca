Orca product direction

Organization UI review

A user-led review of the prototype’s product direction. Technical and accessibility acceptance does not constitute product approval.

Desktop direction explicitly approved

Initial reactions captured

Reduce first-load density

The page feels busy. Keep the rule library and center workbench, but do not permanently expose the full Trace and Audit inspector.

Keep both authoring modes

The text editor and Glass Box are both promising. Glass Box should become more visually expressive and legible.

Preserve simulation evidence

Historical simulation is useful decision data and should remain near the authoring workflow.

Explore a malleable shell

The sidebar direction is stronger than today, but Focus, Signals, Quiet, and Later must support user ownership and movement.

Approved desktop direction

All five desktop decisions are approved. The approved Glass Box is a colorful, whiteboard-like construction surface inspired by Scratch’s buildable clarity: semantic When, If, Then, and Because pieces connect into one causal rule. Mobile remains explicitly deferred.

Agreed direction

01 · Inspector disclosure

Trace and Audit are hidden by default, open contextually in a right drawer, and can be pinned on wide screens with the preference remembered.

02 · Glass Box visual language

The approved revision is colorful, whiteboard-esque, and genuinely buildable, with semantic interlocking pieces rather than four equal panels.

03 · Simulation prominence

A compact result remains attached to the rule, deeper evidence expands on demand, and any edit marks the result stale immediately.

04 · Mobile deferred

Mobile is excluded from this approval and will be reconsidered as the next milestone with its own complete rebranding direction.

05 · Shell and malleable navigation

Stable Orca anchors remain fixed while user-owned workflow spaces can be reordered, renamed, hidden, restored, and eventually created.
