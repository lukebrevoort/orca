## Bounded synchronization experiment after visual acceptance

Luke approved the shown light/dark visual direction ("Looks good to me"); this is not formal PR approval or runtime verification. Third candidate `416f10d99c14984288445f2a6a850a4d0a856fdb` still failed tests 13/42 in both themes: post-gesture animation waits measured 60.03–60.87s, then the next tap waited until the 120s gate expired. Existing screenshots support the appearance, not pending interaction.

The fixture now accepts only named scenarios: `controls` holds at most 240s (three possible 60s waits plus 60s overhead), `switch` at most 360s (five waits plus 60s overhead); default remains 120s. The timer starts at arm and cannot be renewed by polls, reads or claims. Unknown scenarios, duplicate names, arbitrary durations and nonempty arm bodies are rejected. Empty POST streams are accepted after bounded validation (250ms maximum, no whole-body buffering). Sanitized scenario/arm/start/terminal elapsed markers support diagnosis. Expiry remains an error, initial/final pending checks remain strict, and release/recovery remains explicit. Real animations, gestures, taps and production networking are unchanged.

Across both appearances the held-response ceiling is 20 minutes. Third-run test phases took 22.1 minutes with 8 minutes of held-response budget; conservatively adding the 12-minute difference projects 34.1 minutes before setup/build/export variation. Only the focused mailbox job now permits 50 minutes, with a 45-minute test-step cap leaving room for existing cleanup and artifact upload. Signal cleanup is best effort, not a guarantee after forced termination. No automatic further extension is planned. This is an evidence-grounded bounded experiment awaiting native execution; even a pass would establish pending interaction, not latency or responsiveness.
